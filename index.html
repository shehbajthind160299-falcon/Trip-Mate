✦ NEED TO KNOW

Frequently asked questions.

How do I book a trip?

Choose a destination, select transport and passengers, calculate your estimate and add the trip to your cart.

Are these actual travel prices?

No. The prices are sample values created for this educational mini-project.

Does TripMate accept real payments?

No. The checkout only demonstrates a booking confirmation. It does not process payments.
